# Wortgraph Coach — submission video script (~3:00)

Read aloud over the shots. Sign in as **demo**, light mode, dry-run once.

## OPENING — Graph view (20s)
Hi, I'm Rishabh — and this is for submission to the Neo4j Aura Agent
Hackathon: **Wortgraph Coach**, a German vocabulary coach built on a personal
knowledge graph in Neo4j Aura.

Every vocabulary app I've tried stores words as a flat list. But your brain
doesn't store language as a list — it stores it as a network, where you
remember a word through its connections. That mismatch is why flashcards
leak. Wortgraph fixes it by storing your vocabulary the way your brain
actually does: as a graph.

## SHOT 1 — Explore: paste text, extract (25s)
Here's how it works. I paste German I'm actually reading — an article, even
a YouTube transcript. Wortgraph extracts the words worth learning, with
articles, translations and CEFR levels. And every single word is verified
against Wiktionary and the DWDS corpus before it enters the graph — so the
system can never teach me a word an AI made up.

## SHOT 2 — Graph/Clusters: click a node (25s)
Every word is linked to the words it appeared with, and to its topic. After
a few articles, this is what you own — a vocabulary network nobody else has.
Over 250 words, 1,700 connections, live in Neo4j AuraDB. And this isn't a
chart drawn from a table — what you're looking at IS the database.

## SHOT 3 — Arena: Odd-One-Out + "How the graph built this round" (40s)
The graph doesn't just store — it plays. In the Arena, the practice games
are generated by Cypher queries. Odd-One-Out asks the graph for three words
that share a meaning and one outlier — so the puzzle can't hallucinate, and
when I answer, it explains why using the actual relationships. And right
here, I can open the exact Cypher that built the round. The reasoning is
never a black box.

## SHOT 4 — AI Coach: "What new word should I add to my vocabulary next?" (45s)
Now the centerpiece. The AI Coach is a published Neo4j Aura Agent with
twelve tools — ten Cypher templates, a semantic similarity search over
3,072-dimensional Gemini embeddings, and a Text2Cypher fallback. Watch what
happens when I ask what to learn next.

It recommends Dürre — not because it's on some frequency list, but because
the graph found it connects seven words I already know: Verantwortung,
Umwelt, Klimawandel, Erderwärmung. One new word, wiring a whole region of my
vocabulary together. That's a recommendation only a graph can make.

## SHOT 5 — Aura console: tool list + same question (25s)
And this is the same agent living in the Aura console — you can see it
choose the Get Bridge Words tool and traverse the graph in real time. The
web app and the console run on one brain. It's also exposed as an MCP
server — so Claude, or any MCP client, can talk to my vocabulary graph
directly.

## CLOSE — slow zoom out on the graph (15s)
Flashcard apps store your effort as a list and let it leak. Wortgraph
stores it as a graph — where every word you add strengthens the ones you
already have. Wortgraph Coach — built on Neo4j Aura. Thanks for watching.
